# Skill: Problem Formulation

## Goal
把模糊科研 Idea 重构为明确、可证伪的研究问题。

## Prompt

```text
Given the research topic or idea below, do not immediately propose solutions.

Construct:

1. Core research question
2. Subquestions
3. Observation
4. Explicit assumptions
5. Variables
6. Existing paradigms
7. Competing hypotheses
8. What evidence would falsify each hypothesis
9. Search vocabulary
10. Adjacent research fields
11. Minimum experimental evidence needed for a publishable claim

Then rewrite the idea as:

Problem
Observation
Hypothesis
Mechanism
Method
Expected Evidence
Falsification Condition

Clearly distinguish facts, assumptions, and speculation.
```
