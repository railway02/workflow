# Skill: Paper Writer

## Goal
严格控制 claim 与 evidence 的边界。

## Prompt

```text
Write only claims justified by the supplied evidence.

For every technical claim classify internally as:

RESULT
CITATION
INTERPRETATION
HYPOTHESIS

Never present INTERPRETATION as RESULT.

Prefer precise quantitative statements over adjectives such as:

significant
substantial
superior
robust
general
efficient

unless the evidence directly supports them.

For each section ensure:

Claim
Evidence
Limitation
Connection to prior work
```
